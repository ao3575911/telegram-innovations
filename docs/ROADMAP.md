# Roadmap and commercial next step

## Delivered MVP

One threshold commitment card, explicit Join/Leave, automatic deadline outcomes, creator/admin cancellation, scoped membership storage, privacy controls, retention, native deployment, and automated tests. Payments, recurring tasks, AI, Mini Apps, reputation, and attendance tracking are deliberately excluded.

## Next milestone: four-week community pilot

Recruit 10 consenting communities with recurring activities and a minimum-team problem. Complete live Telegram acceptance, then compare the card with each group's existing workflow. Interview organisers after their first and third pact. Record aggregate creation, closure, and repeat-use counts with consent; ask organisers separately about attendance and administrative effort.

Proceed if organisers repeatedly create pacts and report a concrete coordination improvement. Rework the interaction if most pacts are unused, members mistake READY for a guarantee, or public counts create unwanted pressure. Stop expansion if a normal Telegram poll solves the same problem equally well for the target audience.

## After evidence of repeat use

1. **Reliability:** Backup/restore drills, migration fixtures, measured update throughput, live API outage exercises, and operational alerting.
2. **Repeat activity:** Opt-in recurring templates and organiser-managed reminders, with explicit message permissions and clear opt-out.
3. **Commercial pilot:** Managed hosting and support for organisations coordinating multiple communities. Charge for service and reliability before building a payment subsystem.
4. **Optional coordination:** A separately consented contact/roster flow, if interviews show that aggregate-only cards block useful work.
5. **Scale:** PostgreSQL or a work queue only after profiling demonstrates SQLite or one worker is inadequate.

Potential subscription dimensions are supported communities, recurring templates, and aggregate organiser reports. Do not monetise participant data. Exact pricing and conversion targets require buyer discovery. The defensible asset would be trusted group workflow and organiser distribution, not a novel cryptographic primitive or an assumed moat around buttons.
